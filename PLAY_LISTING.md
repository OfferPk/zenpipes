## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

Immerse yourself in tranquil logic and soothing water streams with ZenPipes — an elegant, calming offline pipe flow rotation puzzle game designed to refresh your mind while exercising your spatial reasoning.

Your mission is simple and deeply satisfying: rotate disjointed pipe fittings on the grid to create an unbroken water circuit that carries life from the central fountain into every sacred lantern without any leaks or loose ends.

With 50 handcrafted and solver-verified levels across five progressive difficulty packs — plus an infinite Zen Free Play generator — ZenPipes delivers a serene, mindful puzzle experience.

Whether playing during your daily commute, taking a relaxing pause between tasks, or winding down before sleep, ZenPipes offers an ad-free, completely private offline sanctuary.

### 🌟 Game Highlights:

- **100% Offline Gameplay**: Play freely anywhere — on flights, trains, or remote retreats without needing Wi-Fi or cellular data.
- **50 Curated & Solver-Verified Levels**: Journey through 5 distinct difficulty packs:
  • Pack 1: Spring Awakening (3x3 grids) — Gentle ripples to introduce basic pipe rotation and flow
  • Pack 2: River Crossing (4x4 grids) — Balanced multi-path stream crossings and aqueducts
  • Pack 3: Aqueduct Gate (5x5 grids) — Expansive irrigation channels with branching junctions
  • Pack 4: Temple Conduits (5x5 & 6x6 grids) — Intricate multi-lantern sacred networks
  • Pack 5: Infinite Flow (6x6 grids) — Grand meditative master challenges
- **Zen Free Play Mode**: Generate endless custom solvable flow puzzles on 3x3, 4x4, 5x5, or 6x6 boards whenever you crave a fresh challenge.
- **Real-Time Flow Simulation & Hint Assistant**: Instant BFS flow traversal highlights live water conduits as soon as connections are made. Stuck on a tricky orientation? Tap Hint to reveal the next best rotation.
- **Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer**: Enjoy acoustic bamboo water fountain clicks and crystal droplet chimes synthesized dynamically, crowned by rewarding victory harmonies.
- **4 Radiant Color Themes**: Aquamarine Stream, Emerald Bamboo, Solar Amber, and Amethyst River.
- **Optimal Move Scoring**: Aim for 3 stars on every stage by solving puzzles within the target Par rotation count.
- **Unlimited Undo & Instant Reset**: Explore alternative paths with unrestricted move take-backs and rapid board restarts.
- **Zero Ads, Zero Trackers**: Uninterrupted casual gameplay with no ads, pop-ups, or microtransactions.
