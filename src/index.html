How to Play ZenPipes

Objective

Rotate pipe segments so that every conduit connects back to the water spring without any open leaks facing empty space or walls.

Rotation Mechanics

Tap or click any pipe to rotate it 90° clockwise. Glowing blue streams indicate active water flowing from the fountain source.

Star Ratings & Par

Solve within the target Par rotation count to earn all 3 Stars. Additional moves earn 2 or 1 star.

Keyboard Shortcuts

• Z: Undo rotation
• R: Reset level
• H: Hint assistant
• M: Sound toggle
